# Example clips

These 4 s clips are trimmed from [BabySlakh](https://zenodo.org/records/4603870), by Ethan
Manilow, Gordon Wichern, Prem Seetharaman and Jonathan Le Roux, licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They were mixed down to mono
and peak-normalised by `scripts/make_examples.py`, which lists the track each comes from.
Slakh is described in "Cutting Music Source Separation Some Slakh: A Dataset to Study the
Impact of Training Data Quality and Quantity" (WASPAA 2019).
