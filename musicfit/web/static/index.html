musicfit fit a synth + notes to audio, then play with the patch

Fit a clip

Advanced

A Standard fit of a 3-4 s clip with one voice takes about 20 s on an NVIDIA GPU and several minutes on a CPU; time grows with clip length and voices. Longer runs than Standard fit worse, not better.

Sparsity is the penalty that keeps the piano roll from explaining everything with many quiet notes. Raise it if you get spurious notes, lower it if notes go missing.
